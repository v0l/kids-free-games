# Privacy policy

Kids Free Games does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- No accounts, sign-in or profiles stored on a server. Player names and progress stay on the device.
- No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting.
- No in-app purchases.
- No network access. The app works fully offline.
- No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.
