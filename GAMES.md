## Learning games

| Game | What it teaches |
|---|---|
| Math Pop | Mixed warm-up: adding, taking away and times tables, pop the balloon with the answer |
| Count It | Counting 1 to 20 |
| Spell It | Spelling 30 picture words, 3 to 7 letters |

None of the games here have ads, purchases, accounts, network access or tracking. Nothing leaves the device.

## Planned games

Every planned game follows the rules in `docs/STYLE.md`: painted art, an easy first minute, levels saved per player, stars per level, and the shared results board on game over. Learning games resume at the level the child reached and drop back a step after a run of mistakes.

### Maths

Math Pop stays as the mixed warm-up. Each operation also gets a game of its own, so a child can practise one thing until it sticks.

| Game | Purpose | How it plays | Levels |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Take Away Trains** (subtraction) | Taking away, then subtracting with bigger numbers | A train arrives with passengers in its carriages. The question says "7 take away 3"; the child taps 3 passengers off the train, then picks how many are left. Later levels hide the passengers and just show the sum | 1. Take away within 5 with pictures. 2. Within 10 with pictures. 3. Within 10, pictures fade after a second. 4. Within 20, numbers only. 5. Missing numbers (12 - ? = 7). 6. Two-digit minus one-digit (34 - 6). 7. Two-digit minus two-digit, no borrowing. 8. With borrowing |
| **Times Table Garden** (multiplication) | Times tables 1-12 as groups and arrays | Each answer plants rows of flowers: 3 x 4 grows 3 rows of 4. Tap the right answer from three sprouting seeds; a wrong pick wilts it and shows the array to count. Each mastered table blooms a new bed in the garden | One table per level in teaching order: 2, 10, 5, 3, 4, 8, 6, 9, 7, 11, 12, then mixed. A table counts as mastered at 10 right in a row. Each table has three stages: arrays to count, arrays that fade, numbers only |
| **Share the Treats** (division) | Division as fair sharing and grouping | Drag cookies onto plates so every animal friend gets the same. Then "12 cookies, 3 friends, how many each?" without dragging. Leftovers appear at the remainder levels | 1. Share 6-12 between 2. 2. Share between 2-5 with drag. 3. Share with drag, then answer. 4. Answer only, using times tables the child has mastered in Times Table Garden. 5. Missing divisor (12 / ? = 4). 6. Remainders ("13 between 4: 3 each, 1 left over") |
| **Number Bonds** (addition fluency) | Pairs that make 10 and 20, the base for mental arithmetic | Two pans of a scale: one side has a number, the child picks the weight that balances it to the target | Bonds to 5, 10, 20, then 100 in tens |

### Puzzles

| Game | Purpose | How it plays | Levels |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Jigsaw** | Shape matching, patience, fine motor control | A painted picture splits into pieces scattered round the edge. Drag a piece near its spot and it snaps in. A faint ghost of the picture sits behind the board on early levels. Finished pictures go into a sticker book | Piece counts 4, 6, 9, 12, 16, 25, 36, 49. Early levels use square pieces with a ghost image. Later levels use classic tab-and-blank shapes, no ghost, and pieces that start rotated (tap to turn). Packs: animals, vehicles, space, ocean, dinosaurs, fairy tales; each pack unlocks after the previous one has 3 stars |
| **Sliding Tiles** | Planning moves ahead | The classic 8-puzzle with painted pictures | 2x2, then 3x3, 4x4 and 5x5. Stars for moves under par |
| **Ball Sort** | Sorting and planning (Color Bump / ball-sort apps) | Tap a tube, tap another to pour the top ball. Sort so each tube holds one colour | 3 colours and 5 tubes, growing to 10 colours. Then hidden balls that reveal when they reach the top. Stars for no undo |
| **Pattern Path** | Sequences and patterns | A path of stepping stones in a pattern (red, blue, red, ?); pick the next stone to cross the river | AB, then AAB, ABC, growing numbers (2, 4, 6), shapes plus colours |

### Words

| Game | Purpose | How it plays | Levels |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phonics Pop** | Letter sounds before spelling | The voice says a sound; pop the balloon with the matching letter | Single sounds (s a t p i n), then the rest of the alphabet, digraphs (sh ch th), blends |
